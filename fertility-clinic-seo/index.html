What is fertility clinic SEO?

Fertility clinic SEO is search engine optimization engineered for a YMYL ("Your Money or Your Life") medical category: technical site health, physician-reviewed content with named authors, local and entity signals, and earned authority — built so a fertility clinic ranks for the searches patients make and converts them into consultations. It differs from generic SEO because Google applies stricter E-E-A-T trust standards to fertility content.

Why does YMYL change fertility SEO?

YMYL — "Your Money or Your Life" — is Google's classification for content that can affect health or finances, and fertility sits squarely inside it. Google evaluates fertility sites against stricter E-E-A-T trust signals, so tactics that work in ordinary industries stall or backfire here.

Fertility clinic SEO FAQs

How long does fertility clinic SEO take to work?

Months, not weeks — honestly. Technical fixes and local improvements can move earlier, but earning durable rankings for competitive fertility terms takes sustained content and authority work. The trade-off is that SEO compounds: rankings earned properly keep producing consultations without paying per click.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No — and that protects your clinic. Guaranteed rankings in a YMYL category usually signal shortcuts that risk your reputation and compliance posture. We commit to what we control: a documented blueprint, transparent monthly reporting on leading indicators and consultations, and no long-term lock-in.

Who writes the medical content?

Specialist health writers working inside a physician-review editorial workflow: clinical claims are reviewed, authors are named, and language stays conservative. Your clinicians approve medical accuracy; we engineer the structure, search alignment, and internal linking around it.

What does fertility clinic SEO cost?

Scope and market competitiveness set the price, so we scope from audit findings rather than a rate card. For typical US ranges and the pricing models agencies use, read our fertility clinic marketing cost guide — then get a preliminary read on your scope in a free strategy call.

Can you work with our existing website?

Usually, yes. The audit tells us whether your platform can support technical fixes, content, and schema — most can. If a rebuild genuinely pays back faster, we will show the evidence and the trade-offs in writing, and the site remains yours either way.

How does SEO fit with Google Ads?

They compound. Ads buy demand now and generate search-term data that sharpens SEO targeting; SEO builds durable visibility that lowers your blended cost per consultation over time. Many clinics run both — see our Google Ads for fertility clinics page for how the paid side stays disciplined.
