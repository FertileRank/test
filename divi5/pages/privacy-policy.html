01 Introduction & Scope

MedTech For Solutions Inc. (“MedTech,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a healthcare consulting company founded in 2005 and headquartered at 399 Knollwood Road, White Plains, NY 10603, USA. We provide IVF laboratory management, regulatory compliance, staffing, group purchasing, practice development, and management services to fertility clinics and assisted reproductive technology (ART) facilities.

This Privacy Policy describes how MedTech collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you:

  • Visit medtech4solutions.com (the “Site”);
  • Submit an inquiry, request information, or otherwise communicate with us;
  • Engage MedTech as a vendor, consultant, or service provider;
  • Apply for employment or temporary placement through our staffing services; or
  • Participate in our Group Purchasing Organization (GPO).
About This Policy This Policy is a general statement covering MedTech's relationship with website visitors, prospects, clients, and candidates. It is not a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, and it does not displace any Business Associate Agreement (BAA), service contract, or staffing agreement you may have with us — those documents control where they apply.

By accessing the Site or providing information to MedTech, you acknowledge you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the Site or submit information to us.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically when you use the Site, and information received from third parties such as your employer, recruiter, or referral source.

Information You Provide

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, mailing address, organization, and job title submitted through inquiry forms, email, or phone.
  • Practice details — information about your fertility clinic, IVF laboratory, or facility including size, location, services offered, and current vendors, when shared during scoping discussions.
  • Engagement records — correspondence, meeting notes, project documents, and operational data exchanged during the course of providing our services.
  • Recruitment information — résumés, CVs, professional credentials (TS/ABB certification, HCLD designation, board certifications), employment history, references, and licensing details when you apply for placement.
  • GPO membership data — entity name, federal tax identification number, authorized signatory, billing address, and product purchasing volumes.
  • Payment and financial information — banking information, payment terms, and invoicing addresses for clients and vendors. Credit card data, when accepted, is processed by PCI-compliant third-party processors and is not stored on our servers.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and connection — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, screen resolution, language preference, and time zone.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URL, search terms used to reach the Site, and links clicked.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 6 below.

Information from Third Parties

  • Referrals — basic identifying information when an existing client, partner, or industry contact refers you to us.
  • Recruitment partners — information shared by staffing platforms, professional associations, or licensing bodies in the context of placement.
  • Public sources — information from professional directories, conference attendee lists, regulatory filings, and publicly available business records.

04 HIPAA & Protected Health Information

MedTech is generally not a Covered Entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA). However, several of our services — including Real-Time Laboratory Monitoring and certain practice management engagements — cause us to act as a Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity clients (fertility clinics, hospitals, and physician practices).

Do Not Send PHI Through the Website The Site, our public email addresses, and our web inquiry forms are not appropriate channels for Protected Health Information (PHI). Please do not include patient names, dates of birth, treatment details, or any other PHI in inquiry forms, voicemails, or unencrypted emails. Once an engagement is established, secure channels and a Business Associate Agreement will be put in place where required.

When We Act as a Business Associate

For services in which MedTech receives, creates, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of a Covered Entity, we enter into a written Business Associate Agreement (BAA) prior to receiving any PHI. The BAA, together with HIPAA's Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules, governs our handling of that PHI — not this Privacy Policy. The BAA addresses, among other things:

  • Permitted and required uses and disclosures of PHI;
  • Administrative, physical, and technical safeguards we will maintain;
  • Subcontractor flow-down obligations;
  • Breach notification timelines and procedures;
  • Return or destruction of PHI at termination.

Patient Inquiries

If you are a patient of one of our client clinics, MedTech is not your healthcare provider. Please direct all clinical questions, medical record requests, accounting-of-disclosures requests, and similar HIPAA-related inquiries to your treating clinic. We will route any patient inquiry that reaches us to the appropriate clinic when possible and otherwise instruct the patient to contact their provider directly.

De-Identified and Aggregate Data

We may use de-identified data (data from which all individually identifying elements have been removed in accordance with HIPAA's de-identification standard) and aggregate statistics for benchmarking, research, quality improvement, and the development of our services. De-identified data is not PHI and is not subject to HIPAA restrictions on use or disclosure.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the visitor experience. We do not use the Site for behavioral advertising or programmatic ad targeting.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary — required for basic Site functionality such as session continuity, security, load balancing, and form submission. These cannot be disabled without breaking the Site.
  • Analytics — aggregate, anonymized usage statistics (such as which pages are most-visited and approximate visitor geography) that help us improve content and structure. We currently use a privacy-respecting analytics provider that does not set cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Preferences — small amounts of data stored locally to remember non-sensitive choices like accepted cookie banners or expanded FAQ items.

Your Choices

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when cookies are set. You may also use private/incognito browsing modes, browser-level “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals, or extensions that block analytics. We honor recognized opt-out signals to the extent technically practicable. Disabling cookies may impair certain Site functions.

07 Data Security

MedTech maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, alteration, and destruction. Measures include, as appropriate:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest for systems handling sensitive or regulated information;
  • Role-based access controls and the principle of least privilege;
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access to client-facing platforms;
  • Regular vulnerability scanning, patching, and security review;
  • Vendor security assessments and contractual flow-down of security obligations;
  • Incident response procedures with defined notification timelines, including HIPAA Breach Notification Rule compliance for PHI under our control.

Despite these measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we treat the protection of information entrusted to us with the seriousness our clients and their patients deserve.

08 Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with legal and regulatory obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements. Typical retention periods include:

  • Inquiry and lead records — up to 24 months from last contact, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Engagement records — for the term of the engagement plus a period required by contract, professional standards, and applicable statutes of limitations (typically 6–7 years from the conclusion of the engagement).
  • Recruitment files — 3 years from last activity, unless a longer period is required by law or you have asked to remain in our active candidate pool.
  • Financial and tax records — for periods required by federal and state tax law (generally 7 years).
  • PHI under a BAA — according to the terms of the BAA and applicable HIPAA requirements, including 6-year retention of certain documentation.
