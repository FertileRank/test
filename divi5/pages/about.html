The ART Industry's Most Trusted Partner

MedTech For Solutions is a healthcare consulting company founded in 2005, specializing exclusively in the assisted reproductive technology (ART) industry. Headquartered in White Plains, NY, MedTech provides end-to-end solutions for fertility clinics, IVF laboratories, and reproductive medicine facilities across the United States and internationally.

Our team of laboratory, clinical, risk management, practice development, and marketing experts brings real-world healthcare and practice management experience. We don't just advise — we provide hands-on implementation and management support to achieve measurable results.

Our mission is to assist in building your competitive market advantage, drive your ART laboratory success, and improve your bottom line through customized solutions for practices of all sizes.

In addition, MedTech offers a comprehensive Group Purchasing Organization (GPO) program designed specifically for the fertility and reproductive healthcare industry. Through our established vendor relationships and national purchasing network, participating practices gain access to preferred pricing, volume discounts, and negotiated contracts on critical laboratory, clinical, pharmaceutical, medical supply, and operational products and services.

Our GPO solutions help practices reduce operating expenses, improve purchasing efficiency, and strengthen financial performance without compromising quality or patient care. By leveraging the collective buying power of our network, MedTech enables practices of all sizes to achieve meaningful cost savings while maintaining access to leading technologies, equipment, and service providers within the ART industry.
