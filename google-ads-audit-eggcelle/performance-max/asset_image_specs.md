# Performance Max — image / logo / video asset specs

Text assets are provided in `pmax_text_assets.csv`. The visual assets below are **binary creative**
and must be supplied by you (or your designer) — they can't be generated as text/CSV. PMax will not
serve across all channels without them (and will lean on auto-generated/low-quality fallbacks if
they're missing), so treat this as a checklist per asset group.

## Images (per asset group)
| Type | Ratio | Recommended size | Min | Recommended |
|------|-------|------------------|-----|-------------|
| Landscape | 1.91:1 | 1200 × 628 | 1 | 3–5 |
| Square | 1:1 | 1200 × 1200 | 1 | 3–5 |
| Portrait | 4:5 | 960 × 1200 | 0 | 2–3 (improves Discover/Gmail) |

- Up to **20** images per asset group · max **5120 KB** each · min width 600 px.
- Avoid heavy text overlay (Google may down-rank or crop). Show real, warm, diverse imagery of
  women 20s–early 30s, supportive/clinical-but-friendly settings.

## Logos (per asset group)
| Type | Ratio | Recommended size | Min |
|------|-------|------------------|-----|
| Square | 1:1 | 1200 × 1200 | 1 |
| Landscape | 4:1 | 1200 × 300 | 0 (recommended) |

- Transparent PNG preferred · max 5120 KB.

## Video (per asset group)
- **Optional but strongly recommended.** ≥ **10 seconds**, hosted on YouTube (provide the URL).
- Vertical (9:16) and/or square (1:1) perform well on the video/Discover surfaces.
- If you provide none, PMax auto-generates a video from your images — lower quality; add a real one
  when you can.

## Practical minimum to launch a healthy asset group
- 1 landscape + 1 square + 1 portrait image (3+ each is better)
- 1 square logo (+ landscape logo)
- 1 short YouTube video (10–30s)
- All the text assets from `pmax_text_assets.csv`

## Reuse
Use the same core asset library across MI/OH/NC and swap any location-specific imagery (e.g., a local
clinic photo) per asset group. Keep brand, colors, and CTA consistent with the landing page.
