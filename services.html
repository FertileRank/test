FAQ

Questions we hear from new partners

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. About a third of our partners start with SEO + GBP, prove the model, and expand into paid and CRO from there. We’ll never sell you scope you don’t need yet.

Do you handle paid media management fees on top?

No hidden fees. Management is included in the retainer. You only pay the platforms (Google, Meta, etc.) for media spend.

Who owns the work we produce together?

You do. Every page, asset, account, and analytics property belongs to your business from day one.

Can you guarantee specific ranking outcomes?

No reputable agency can, and any that does is bluffing. What we can guarantee: a published 90-day roadmap, weekly transparency on what we’re shipping, and measurable progress against the KPIs we agree on.

What does “HIPAA-aware” actually mean?

We don’t process or store PHI. We design your marketing stack so PHI never leaks into analytics, paid platforms, or third-party scripts — using server-side tagging, consent management, and BAA-eligible vendors where required.
