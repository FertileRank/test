About MedTech For Solutions

The ART Industry's Most Trusted Partner

MedTech For Solutions, founded in 2005, is a healthcare company specializing in the assisted reproductive technology (ART) industry. Based in White Plains, NY, we deliver comprehensive solutions for fertility clinics, IVF labs, and reproductive medicine centers across the U.S. and internationally.

Our team of laboratory, clinical, risk management, revenue cycle management, and marketing specialists brings practical experience in ART and practice management. We offer hands-on implementation and management support that enhances your competitive edge, drives laboratory success, and boosts your profitability.

In addition, MedTech provides a Group Purchasing Organization (GPO) tailored for the fertility sector. Through our vendor partnerships and national purchasing network, practices enjoy preferred pricing and volume discounts on essential products and services.

By leveraging our collective buying power, MedTech helps ART practices of all sizes reduce operational costs and access advanced technologies, ultimately improving financial outcomes without sacrificing patient care.

MedTech For Solutions is a healthcare consulting company founded in 2005, specializing exclusively in the assisted reproductive technology (ART) industry. Based in White Plains, NY, MedTech provides IVF laboratory management, practice development, temporary staffing, recruitment, regulatory compliance, and group purchasing services to fertility clinics and IVF laboratories across the United States and internationally.

MedTech provides comprehensive IVF laboratory services including laboratory design and construction, real-time monitoring via the OvaTools platform, regulatory compliance for FDA, CLIA, CAP, and AABB, quality control programs, temporary staffing with TS (ABB) certified professionals, group purchasing through 1,800+ vendor contracts, and practice development consulting. MedTech has designed or managed over 30 ART laboratories nationally.

MedTech's GPO delivers comprehensive Group Purchasing Organization solutions designed exclusively for fertility practices. By pooling the collective buying power of over 300 IVF member practices, the GPO secures significantly lower pricing across a broad range of laboratory supplies, clinical products, and operational services — savings that would be impossible to achieve individually.

The GPO is free to join with no purchasing obligations or spending commitments. Members access 1,800+ active vendor contracts with leaders including Staples, FedEx, Roche, Nikon, Olympus, and GE. GPO members also receive 10% - 50% cost savings on MedTech professional services, depending on the situation.

Participation in MedTech's GPO means your practice benefits from the negotiating leverage of an entire network of fertility clinics — reducing costs on everything from everyday office supplies and shipping to specialized laboratory reagents and equipment, freeing resources to reinvest in patient care and practice growth.
