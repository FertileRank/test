Common questions about your first visit

Everything you need to know before requesting an appointment.

How quickly will I get a response?
Our patient navigator responds within one business day. Most new patients are scheduled for a first consultation within 1–2 weeks of submitting this form.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Bring your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of current medications, and any prior fertility records, imaging, or labs. We'll send a secure portal link in advance so you can upload records ahead of time.
Will my insurance be verified first?
Yes. Our financial counselors verify your benefits before your first visit and walk you through exactly what's covered, what isn't, and what your out-of-pocket estimate will be.
Do I need a referral?
Most patients can self-refer and do not need a referral. Some HMO plans require one from your OB-GYN or primary care provider — we'll let you know when we verify your benefits.
Can my partner attend with me?
Absolutely — we encourage it. Your partner is welcome at every visit, and we'll evaluate both partners as part of a comprehensive fertility workup when applicable.
Is this an emergency line?
No. This form is for new-patient appointment requests only. If you are an existing patient with an urgent medical concern, please call (248) 619-3100. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911.
