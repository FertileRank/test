Implementation notes for dev handoff

Conversion tracking: Replace AW-XXXXXXXXX in the gtag snippets (currently commented out at the top of each file) with the live Google Ads conversion ID and label. Uncomment the snippets and the gtag('event','conversion',…) call inside the form submit handler.

Form endpoint: Each form posts to /api/leads with hidden fields campaign_id, location, and partner_clinic so leads can be routed by campaign in your CRM.

Phone number: All pages use the EggCelle main line (866) 230-0094. Swap for tracked numbers if call attribution is desired.

Clinic logos: Each partner block currently shows a styled wordmark placeholder. Drop the partner clinic logo PNG/SVG into .clinic-logo-placeholder.

SEO: All pages are noindex, nofollow — these are paid-only landing pages, never organic.
