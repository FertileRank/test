Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies the FertileRank website uses, what they do, and how you can control them. It works alongside our Privacy Policy, which covers how we handle personal information more broadly.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. They let a site remember things between page views — such as a session, a preference, or, with analytics, which pages were visited. Cookies set by the site you are visiting are "first-party"; cookies set by other services are "third-party."

Cookies this site uses

  • Essential cookies (always on). Required for the site to function: session handling and remembering your cookie-consent choice. These cannot be switched off through the consent banner because the site does not work properly without them.
  • Analytics cookies (only with your consent). Used to understand how visitors move through the site — pages viewed, time on page, and referral source — so we can improve the content. Analytics tools load only after you accept them in the consent banner.

We do not place advertising or retargeting pixels on this site without your consent. If that ever changes for a specific campaign, those technologies will load only after an explicit opt-in through the consent banner, and this policy will be updated to name them.

How consent works

This site sets nothing beyond essential cookies until you consent. When analytics is enabled, a consent banner asks on your first visit whether you accept non-essential cookies; no analytics cookie is set until you choose. Your choice is stored so we do not ask on every page, and you can change it at any time.

How to opt out

  • Consent banner. Decline analytics cookies when the banner appears. To withdraw consent you gave earlier, clear this site's cookies in your browser — your choice resets and the banner asks again on your next visit.
  • Browser settings. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, either for all sites or site by site. Check your browser's privacy or settings menu. Blocking essential cookies may affect how this site functions.

Changes to this policy

If the cookies or analytics tools we use change, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
