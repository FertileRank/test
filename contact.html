FAQ

Quick answers

Do I have to be ready to sign before we talk?

Not at all. The strategy call is a no-strings conversation. About a third of the brands we talk to aren’t ready for an agency yet — we’ll still send the three-ideas memo.

Will I get a real strategist or an SDR?

A real strategist. We don’t use sales-development reps, and we don’t outsource discovery. The person you talk to will be on your account if we end up working together.

I’m an existing client — should I use this form?

Please ping your account team directly in Slack for anything urgent. For new scope conversations, this form is fine.

Can we just see a proposal first?

Honestly, no — a proposal we’d send without a 30-minute conversation would be a generic one, and we don’t do those. The call is worth your time.
