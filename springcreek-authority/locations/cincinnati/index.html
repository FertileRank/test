Cincinnati fertility clinic FAQs

Where is SpringCreek’s Cincinnati IVF clinic located?

Our Cincinnati-area clinic is at 9313 Mason Montgomery Road, Suite 225, Mason, OH 45040, just off I-71 and convenient to West Chester, Liberty Township, and Loveland. Call (513) 457-5200 to schedule.

What infertility support does SpringCreek offer in Cincinnati?

Beyond medical treatment, our Cincinnati team provides clear education about your diagnosis and options, a dedicated care team you can reach with questions, and compassionate guidance through every decision — so you feel supported emotionally as well as clinically.

Does the Mason clinic offer IVF and egg freezing?

Yes. Our Cincinnati IVF clinic offers the full continuum of care, including IVF, IUI, comprehensive fertility testing, egg and embryo freezing, preimplantation genetic testing (PGT), and donor and third-party reproduction.

Do I need a referral to be seen in Cincinnati?

No referral is required — you can self-refer and book directly. We also accept referrals from area OB-GYNs and primary care providers. Because fertility coverage varies, we suggest confirming your specific insurance benefits before your first visit.

When is the right time to see a fertility specialist?

Consider scheduling if you’re under 35 and have tried to conceive for 12 months, or 6 months if you’re 35 or older. Don’t wait if you have irregular cycles, known conditions like PCOS or endometriosis, prior pregnancy losses, or simply want to understand your options.
