About

We’re a category-only agency for the most personal category there is.

FertileRank exists because growing a fertility brand is nothing like growing a SaaS company — and the marketing playbooks shouldn’t be either.

Our story

Started in a fertility clinic’s waiting room.

FertileRank was founded in 2021 by Kevin Liu (former growth lead at a Charlotte IVF network) and Dr. Priya Sharma (reproductive endocrinologist turned health-tech founder). They’d watched four agencies cycle through the clinic in three years — each one promising the world and failing to understand the basics of the patient journey, the regulatory landscape, or the emotional weight of what they were marketing.

So they started something different: an agency staffed by people who’d worked inside fertility — clinicians, donor-recruitment leads, embryologists, patient-experience designers — and trained them in modern growth marketing instead of the other way around.

Five years later we’re a 38-person team working with 42 fertility brands across the US, Canada, and the UK. We’re still picky about who we take on — and we still think this is the most meaningful work in marketing.

What we believe

Five principles that shape every engagement

①

Focus beats breadth

One category, done extraordinarily well. We say no to ecommerce, SaaS, real estate — everything outside reproductive health.

②

Clinicians shape the work

Every piece of editorial passes through a medical reviewer. Every paid creative is gut-checked by someone who’s sat with patients.

③

Show our work weekly

You don’t wait until month-end to learn what we shipped. Slack updates, Loom walkthroughs, live dashboards — always on.

④

Boring metrics that matter

Booked consults. Verified applications. Revenue. We don’t pad reports with impressions or reach.

⑤

Empathy is a competitive edge

Our audience is making one of the hardest decisions of their lives. Marketing that respects that always outperforms marketing that doesn’t.

⑥

Earn the renewal

We’re month-to-month after the first quarter. If we’re not delivering value, you should leave — and we’ll help you transition cleanly.
