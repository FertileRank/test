# Visual Asset Recommendations — SpringCreek Fertility Homepage

Brand palette referenced throughout: **Primary Teal `#007F8B` / `#1A7B7E`**, **Dark Teal `#00616B`**,
**Light Teal `#E8F6F7`**, **Cream `#FCF9F0` / `#FDFBF7`**, **Navy `#2D3436`**, **Blue accents `#60B9E5` / `#A7D8F1`**.

> Each asset lists: **Placement · Description · Purpose/SEO Value · Suggested Alt Text (<125 chars) · File Format & Size.**
> Use authentic, consent-based imagery. Avoid stock that implies a specific clinical outcome or excludes diverse family structures.

---

### 1. Hero Background Image
- **Placement:** Behind the hero headline/sub-headline (`.scf-hero`).
- **Description:** Soft, hopeful lifestyle photo of a diverse adult (or two adults) in warm natural light, looking forward with calm optimism near a sunlit window. Composition keeps the left third low-detail so headline text stays legible. Color-graded toward light teal and cream to match the gradient.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Sets an emotionally resonant, inclusive first impression; reduces bounce; ranks in image search for "fertility clinic Ohio."
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `Hopeful adults in warm light considering fertility care at SpringCreek Fertility in Ohio`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP, 1920×1080, **under 150 KB** (compressed); provide 2× and mobile crop.

### 2. "Your First Visit" Supporting Photo
- **Placement:** Inside or beside the hero "What to expect" card.
- **Description:** Candid, friendly photo of a reproductive endocrinologist speaking with a patient across a desk, both relaxed; teal accent wall or scrubs. Conveys clear explanations and a supportive environment.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Reinforces the patient-centered message; builds trust at the conversion point.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `Reproductive endocrinologist explaining fertility options to a patient at SpringCreek Fertility`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP, 800×600, under 90 KB.

### 3. Physician Portrait — Dr. Jeremy Groll
- **Placement:** Why Choose SpringCreek (leadership card) and as the `Physician` schema image.
- **Description:** Professional, approachable headshot of Dr. Jeremy Groll, Medical Director, in a navy or teal blazer/white coat against a clean cream background; soft, even lighting; genuine smile.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Strengthens E-E-A-T (named, credentialed author/provider); supports knowledge-panel and "reproductive endocrinologist Dayton" intent.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `Dr. Jeremy Groll, double board-certified reproductive endocrinologist and Medical Director, SpringCreek Fertility`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP, 600×600, under 70 KB.

### 4. Care Team Group Photo
- **Placement:** Why Choose SpringCreek or a "Meet the team" strip.
- **Description:** Warm group photo of the SpringCreek physicians, nurses, and care coordinators in coordinated teal/navy, natural light, genuine expressions. Reflects the real, multidisciplinary team.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Humanizes the practice; signals depth of support; boosts dwell time.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `SpringCreek Fertility care team of physicians and nurses serving Dayton, Columbus and Cincinnati`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP, 1400×800, under 160 KB.

### 5. Services Iconography Set (custom line icons)
- **Placement:** Services cards (replaces the placeholder glyphs).
- **Description:** Cohesive set of 6 minimalist line icons (Evaluation, IVF, IUI, Egg Freezing, Donor/Third-Party, LGBTQ+ Family Building) drawn in Dark Teal `#00616B` on Light Teal `#E8F6F7` rounded tiles; 2px stroke, consistent corner radius.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Improves scannability and visual hierarchy; lightweight; reinforces brand system.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** (decorative — use empty `alt=""` if paired with visible card heading) or `Egg freezing service icon`
- **File Format & Size:** Inline SVG (preferred) or WebP sprite, each under 8 KB.

### 6. Patient Journey Process Graphic
- **Placement:** Patient Journey section header or as a horizontal banner.
- **Description:** Clean 6-step horizontal flow (Reach out → Health review → Testing → Plan → Treatment & monitoring → Ongoing support) with numbered teal nodes and connecting line on a cream background; mobile version stacks vertically.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Communicates the process at a glance; reduces uncertainty; can earn image-pack visibility for "fertility process" queries.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `Six-step SpringCreek Fertility patient journey from first visit to ongoing support`
- **File Format & Size:** SVG (scales crisply) or WebP, under 60 KB.

### 7. Three-Location Ohio Map Graphic
- **Placement:** Locations & Contact section.
- **Description:** Stylized map of southern/central Ohio with three teal pins labeled Dayton (Centerville), Columbus (Dublin), and Cincinnati (Mason); subtle county outlines for Montgomery, Franklin, Warren; cream background, navy labels.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Reinforces multi-metro local relevance; supports local-pack and "near me" intent; pairs with `LocalBusiness` geo schema.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `Map of SpringCreek Fertility locations in Dayton, Columbus and Cincinnati, Ohio`
- **File Format & Size:** SVG or WebP, under 80 KB.

### 8. LGBTQ+ Family Building Lifestyle Image
- **Placement:** Services (LGBTQ+ card) or a dedicated inclusive-care band.
- **Description:** Authentic, joyful photo of an LGBTQ+ couple or a single parent by choice in a bright home setting; natural light; no clinical staging. Color-graded to brand palette.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Visually affirms inclusive care; supports "LGBTQ+ family building Ohio" intent; signals welcome to diverse family structures.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `LGBTQ+ couple exploring inclusive family-building options at SpringCreek Fertility in Ohio`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP, 1000×667, under 120 KB.

### 9. Egg Freezing / Fertility Preservation Conceptual Image
- **Placement:** Services (Egg Freezing card) or FAQ band.
- **Description:** Tasteful conceptual image — e.g., a calm patient reviewing a planner/calendar, or a softly lit close-up of cryostorage labeling — emphasizing planning ahead. Avoid graphic lab imagery and avoid implying any specific outcome.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Supports "egg freezing Dayton" / "fertility preservation Ohio" image search; communicates forward planning.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `Patient planning egg freezing and fertility preservation with SpringCreek Fertility in Ohio`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP, 800×533, under 100 KB.

### 10. Trust / Credentials Badge Strip
- **Placement:** Trust & Credibility bar (`.scf-trust`).
- **Description:** Row of simple, monochrome credibility badges on the navy bar: "Est. 2014," "SART Member," "Double Board-Certified," "3 Ohio Locations," "Inclusive Care." Blue-accent dividers; white/blue-light text.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Surfaces E-E-A-T signals instantly; increases conversion confidence.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `SpringCreek Fertility credentials: established 2014, SART member, board-certified, three Ohio locations`
- **File Format & Size:** Inline SVG set or WebP, under 30 KB total.

### 11. Open Graph / Social Share Image
- **Placement:** `og:image` (add to `<head>` once produced).
- **Description:** 1200×630 branded card with the SpringCreek logo, headline "Compassionate Fertility Care for Every Family in Ohio," and the teal-to-cream gradient; readable at small sizes.
- **Purpose / SEO Value:** Controls how the homepage appears when shared on social/messaging; improves click-through.
- **Suggested Alt Text:** `SpringCreek Fertility — compassionate fertility care across Dayton, Columbus and Cincinnati`
- **File Format & Size:** WebP or PNG, 1200×630, under 200 KB.

---

**Accessibility & SEO notes:** Every meaningful image needs descriptive, keyword-aware alt text under
125 characters; purely decorative icons should use `alt=""`. Lazy-load below-the-fold images, define
explicit `width`/`height` to prevent layout shift (CLS), and serve responsive `srcset` sources.
