## 5. Keyword Gaps Addressed (vs. current homepage)

- **Geo-specificity:** current homepage leads with "Ohio, Indiana, Kentucky & Beyond" but under-uses
  metro/neighborhood/county modifiers. Enhanced page adds Centerville, Dublin, Mason, and county/region terms.
- **Question-based content:** current FAQ is shallow; enhanced page adds 10 voice/LLM-optimized Q&As with schema.
- **Cost transparency intent:** "IVF cost Ohio" and financing queries now have a dedicated section + FAQ.
- **Inclusive-search intent:** "LGBTQ+ family building Ohio" and "single parent by choice" now explicitly targeted.
