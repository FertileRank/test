Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Does egg donation affect my future fertility?
No. Egg donation does not reduce your remaining egg supply or impact your future fertility. Your body naturally loses far more eggs each cycle than the ones we retrieve. Your fertility is fully evaluated during screening.
Will I have to pay for anything?
No. All medical screenings, medications, monitoring appointments, anesthesia, and the retrieval procedure are paid for by EggCelle and our partner clinics. Travel reimbursement is included if you live outside the Charlotte metro.
How private is the process?
Fully confidential. Your application and medical records are HIPAA-protected. Intended parents only see a non-identifying donor profile. You can choose anonymous or known donation.
How long does each cycle take?
From your first call to retrieval, plan for 4–8 weeks. The active medication phase is roughly 10–12 days. Recovery is typically 24–48 hours.
What if I start and want to stop?
You can withdraw at any time, for any reason. There is no penalty, no pressure, no obligation. Your wellbeing comes first.
How is the compensation paid?
Compensation is paid in full after a successful retrieval — usually within 3–7 business days via direct deposit. It is reported as 1099 income.
