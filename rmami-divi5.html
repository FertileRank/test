Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Michigan patients ask first.

What fertility services does RMA of Michigan offer?
We provide a full continuum of reproductive care: IVF, IUI, egg freezing and embryo banking, preimplantation genetic testing (PGT-A/M/SR), donor egg and gestational carrier programs, LGBTQ+ family-building, fertility surgery, ovulation induction, and male fertility evaluation — all delivered from our Troy, Michigan clinic.
Where is RMA of Michigan located?
130 Town Center Drive, Suite 106, Troy, MI 48084 — convenient to Detroit, Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills, Royal Oak, Rochester Hills, and the surrounding Metro Detroit area. We see patients from across Michigan.
Does RMA of Michigan accept insurance?
Yes. We're in-network with most major Michigan plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, and Priority Health. Our financial counselors verify benefits before your first appointment and walk you through every coverage detail.
How successful is IVF at RMA of Michigan?
Our IVF success rates consistently exceed national averages reported by SART. Since 2006 we've helped welcome more than 5,000 babies. Live-birth rates vary by age and diagnosis — your physician will share rates specific to your situation during consultation.
How do I schedule a first appointment?
Call (248) 619-3100 or request a consultation online. Most new patients are seen within 1–2 weeks. Bring prior medical records, current medications, and your insurance card.
Do I need a referral to see a fertility specialist?
Most patients do not need a referral and can self-refer directly. Some HMO plans require one from your OB-GYN or primary care provider — our team verifies this when we confirm your benefits.

Ready to take the next step?

Schedule a consultation with a board-certified reproductive endocrinologist. Most new patients are seen within 1–2 weeks — and we'll verify your insurance before you walk in.
