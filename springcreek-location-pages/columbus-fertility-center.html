Columbus fertility clinic FAQs

Where are SpringCreek’s Columbus fertility specialists located?

Our Columbus-area clinic is at 6760 Avery-Muirfield Drive, Suite A, Dublin, OH 43016, just minutes from Columbus, Hilliard, Powell, and Worthington with on-site parking. Call (614) 401-4113 to schedule.

Do you offer IVF and IUI near Columbus?

Yes. Our Dublin location provides the full range of fertility treatment near Columbus, including IUI, IVF, fertility testing, egg and embryo freezing, preimplantation genetic testing (PGT), and donor and third-party reproduction.

Do I need a referral to see a Columbus fertility specialist?

No referral is needed — you can book directly with us. We also welcome referrals from Central Ohio OB-GYNs and primary care providers. We recommend confirming your insurance benefits, as coverage for fertility services varies by plan.

Is the Columbus clinic part of the same practice as Dayton?

Yes. The Columbus (Dublin) clinic is part of SpringCreek Fertility, a physician-owned practice with locations across Ohio. Patients benefit from the same physician leadership, lab standards, and coordinated, continuous care at every location.

When should I schedule a fertility consultation?

Consider an evaluation if you’re under 35 and have been trying to conceive for 12 months, or 6 months if you’re 35 or older. Reach out sooner with irregular cycles, conditions such as PCOS or endometriosis, prior losses, or if you’d like to plan ahead with egg freezing.
