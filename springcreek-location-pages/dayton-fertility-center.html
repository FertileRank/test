Dayton fertility clinic FAQs

Where is SpringCreek Fertility located in Dayton?

Our Dayton fertility clinic is at 7095 Clyo Road, Dayton, OH 45459, in Washington Township near Centerville and Kettering, with easy access from I-675 and free on-site parking. Call (937) 458-5084 to schedule.

Do I need a referral to see a fertility specialist in Dayton?

No referral is required — you can self-refer and book directly with us. We also gladly accept referrals from OB-GYNs and primary care providers across Greater Dayton. Many insurance plans don’t require a referral for an initial fertility consultation, but we recommend confirming your specific benefits.

What fertility treatments are available at the Dayton clinic?

Our Dayton center offers complete fertility care, including comprehensive fertility testing, ovulation induction, intrauterine insemination (IUI), in vitro fertilization (IVF), egg and embryo freezing, preimplantation genetic testing (PGT), and donor and third-party reproduction — all supported by an on-site IVF laboratory.

How much does IVF cost in Dayton, Ohio?

The cost of IVF varies based on your individual treatment plan, medications, and any added services such as genetic testing. At your consultation, our team provides clear, itemized pricing and reviews insurance coverage and financing options so you can plan with confidence. Please contact us at (937) 458-5084 for a personalized estimate.

When should I see a fertility specialist?

Consider an evaluation if you’re under 35 and have been trying to conceive for 12 months, or 6 months if you’re 35 or older. You should also reach out sooner if you have irregular cycles, known reproductive conditions (such as PCOS or endometriosis), prior pregnancy losses, or are planning ahead with egg or embryo freezing.
