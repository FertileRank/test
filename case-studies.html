Featured case

EggCelle — from 38 to 412 monthly donor applications

A regional egg donor program that needed to fill capacity at three NC clinics — without compromising on donor quality or compliance.

The challenge

When EggCelle came to us in 2024, they were averaging 38 donor applications per month across North Carolina. Their cost per qualified applicant on Google Ads had crept above $400. Their site ranked for almost nothing in organic. And they were turning down intended-parent contracts because they couldn’t source enough screened donors fast enough.

What we did

  • ✓ Rebuilt site architecture around state and city pages (NC, Charlotte, Raleigh, Greensboro)
  • ✓ Replaced legacy 14-field application with a 3-step donor prescreen
  • ✓ Shipped a donor-intent Google Ads program with $0.94 average CPL
  • ✓ TikTok & Meta UGC creative featuring real prior donors
  • ✓ Editorial program: 36 long-form articles, all medically reviewed
