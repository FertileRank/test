Common questions

Answers before you ask

Do you only work with fertility brands?

Yes — exclusively. Every team member has shipped work in this category. That focus is why we move faster than horizontal agencies and why our content passes clinical review the first time.

How quickly will I see results?

Most paid-search engagements are profitable within 30 days. SEO compounding kicks in around month 3, with most clients hitting their first major milestone (a doubling of organic inquiries) inside 6 months.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Monthly retainers between $7.5k and $35k depending on scope. Most clients start with an SEO + GBP foundation and add paid search and CRO as the program matures. See our services page for detail.

Are you HIPAA-compliant?

We don’t directly handle PHI, but we configure your marketing stack to be HIPAA-aware end-to-end: server-side GTM, BAA-covered vendors, redacted call tracking, and consent-managed analytics.

Can you work with our existing in-house team?

Absolutely. About 60% of our engagements augment in-house teams. We’ll define a clear RACI in week one so nobody is duplicating work.

Do you have a minimum contract length?

Three months for the initial sprint, then month-to-month. We’d rather earn your renewal than lock you in.
