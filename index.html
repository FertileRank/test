Frequently Asked Questions

What makes FertileRank different from other healthcare marketing agencies?

Focus and structure. FertileRank works only with fertility and reproductive health organizations, so the playbooks are already built for YMYL scrutiny, patient privacy, and long consideration journeys. Every engagement runs on a documented blueprint, clients own all accounts and data, and reporting is tied to consultations — not traffic or impressions.

Do you guarantee results?

No — and that protects your clinic. Guaranteed rankings or lead volumes in a YMYL category usually signal shortcuts that put your reputation and compliance posture at risk. Instead, we commit to what we control: a documented blueprint, transparent monthly reporting on leading indicators and consultations, and no long-term lock-in — so we re-earn the engagement with evidence, not promises.

Is your process safe for patient privacy?

It is designed to be. We run privacy-first workflows designed around HIPAA obligations: no PHI in marketing forms, analytics, or ad platforms; consent-based communication; and BAAs where required. Your medical director can review the data-handling standards before anything ships.

How fast can an engagement start?

Quickly, because the sequence is fixed. We begin with a strategy call, then a growth audit of your visibility, paid accounts, and intake, then a documented blueprint. That sequence typically takes weeks, not months — and you keep the audit findings either way.

Who is a good fit for FertileRank?

US-based IVF and fertility clinics, REI practices, egg donor and surrogacy programs, cryobanks, and OB/GYN groups that want engineered systems and transparent reporting. If you are looking for guaranteed rankings or overnight results, we are not the right partner — and we will say so on the first call.

How much does fertility clinic marketing cost?

It depends on scope — how many systems are in play and how competitive your market is. FertileRank scopes engagements as monthly retainers built from audit findings, so you see the documented blueprint and its priorities before committing, and there is no long-term lock-in without cause. The strategy call is free and includes a preliminary read on scope.
