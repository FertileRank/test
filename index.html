Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions women in North Carolina ask us most.

How much do egg donors get paid in North Carolina?

Qualified EggCelle donors in NC earn up to $10,000 per completed cycle, including a base payment of $8,000 and bonuses for repeat donations. All medical screening, medications, and travel are covered separately.

How long does the egg donation process take?

From application to retrieval, the full process takes about 2–3 months. The active medication portion lasts 10–12 days, and the retrieval procedure itself is completed in under 30 minutes.

Is egg donation safe?

Yes. Egg donation is a well-established medical procedure. Our partner clinic, Reproductive Specialists of the Carolinas, is led by board-certified reproductive endocrinologists who follow ASRM safety guidelines. You’ll be monitored closely throughout your cycle.

Will my egg donation be confidential?

Absolutely. Your identity is protected throughout the process, and donor information is shared only as agreed upon in your contract. Most donations are anonymous.

Where are the appointments held?

All medical appointments take place at our partner clinic in Charlotte, NC. We reimburse travel from anywhere in North Carolina, and lodging is provided when needed.

Will donating affect my future fertility?

No. Research shows that egg donation does not reduce a donor’s natural egg supply or future fertility. Each month your body naturally selects multiple eggs — donation simply rescues eggs that would otherwise be reabsorbed.
