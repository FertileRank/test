The Livonia location offers the same full range of fertility services — IVF, IUI, egg freezing, donor egg, genetic testing, and more — delivered by the same physician team that built our Troy practice.

Why We Expanded

Closer to the families we serve

For 18 years, RMA of Michigan patients have traveled from every corner of southeast Michigan to our Troy clinic. As demand grew — especially from Wayne County, Ann Arbor, and the western metro — we knew we needed to bring our care closer to where families actually live.

The Livonia office is the answer. Same evidence-informed protocols. Same board-certified reproductive endocrinologists. Same on-site embryology and andrology lab work supported from Troy. A shorter drive for thousands of patients who deserve it.

If you've already been seen at Troy, you can choose whichever location is closer for monitoring visits — your records, your team, and your care plan follow you between offices.

Common Questions

About the Livonia office

When did the Livonia office open?

The Livonia office is now open and accepting both new patients and current Troy patients who'd prefer a closer location for monitoring visits or follow-up care.

Can I switch from the Troy office to Livonia?

Yes. If you're already a Troy patient, you can choose Livonia for some or all of your visits. Your records, your physician, and your care plan transfer seamlessly between locations. Just let your nurse coordinator know which office works better for an upcoming visit.

Will I see the same physician at both locations?

Yes. Our five board-certified reproductive endocrinologists rotate between both offices. The physician who builds your protocol is the physician you'll continue to see — at whichever location you're scheduled.

Where does the embryology lab work happen?

Our embryology and andrology lab remains on-site at the Troy clinic, supporting both locations. Eggs, sperm, and embryos are handled by the same dedicated lab team. Meet the lab team →

Do you accept the same insurance at Livonia?

Yes — all the insurance plans we work with at Troy are accepted at Livonia. Our financial counselors verify benefits before your first appointment regardless of which office you choose.

How do I schedule at the Livonia office?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment online — when our patient navigator follows up, just let them know you'd like to be seen in Livonia.
