Should fertility clinics run Google Ads?

Yes — when capacity exists and the account is run with discipline. Google Ads for fertility clinics buys high-intent demand while SEO compounds, but fertility clicks are expensive and healthcare policy limits what campaigns can do, so undisciplined accounts burn budget fast. The formula that works: tight structure, aggressive negative keywords, aligned landing pages, and privacy-safe tracking measured to consultations.

Why is fertility PPC different?

Three constraints separate fertility advertising from ordinary lead generation — and each one punishes generic account templates.

Healthcare policy limits

Google restricts how health campaigns target and personalize. Fertility clinic advertising has to be structured around those limits from day one — not retrofitted after disapprovals.

Privacy constraints

Condition-based remarketing and PHI-driven audiences are off the table in responsible healthcare marketing. We build privacy-first: no patient data in the platform, consent-based measurement throughout.

Expensive clicks

Fertility terms are among the pricier healthcare keywords in competitive metros. When single clicks cost real money, waste compounds quickly — and discipline pays for itself.

Where do fertility ad budgets leak?

The audit almost always finds the same five holes:

  • Broad match sprawl. Loose matching quietly buys searches no patient of yours ever makes.

  • Missing negative keywords. Job seekers, students, and DIY researchers click too — negatives are the discipline that stops paying for them.

  • Generic landing pages. Sending IVF searches to the homepage taxes every conversion. Each campaign needs an aligned page.

  • No call tracking. Fertility patients call. Without privacy-safe call measurement, half the account's results are invisible.

  • Reporting stops at the click. If the scorecard never reaches consultations, optimization chases the wrong number.

Google Ads for fertility clinics: FAQs

What's the minimum ad budget for a fertility clinic?

It depends on your market's click costs and how many service lines you're promoting — there is no universal floor. The honest test: enough budget to generate statistically meaningful search-term data within a month. The audit sizes that number for your metro before you commit to anything.

How fast do ads work compared to SEO?

Ads produce signal in days and consultations in weeks, but efficiency takes optimization cycles — search-term pruning, copy tests, landing improvements. SEO takes months and then compounds. Most growing clinics run both: ads for immediate volume while fertility clinic SEO matures.

Is remarketing allowed for fertility clinics?

Google restricts personalized advertising for sensitive health categories, and responsible clinics go further: we avoid condition-based remarketing and never build audiences from patient data. Growth comes from intent-based search, strong landing pages, and follow-up done through consented channels — not from following patients around the internet.

Do we keep the ad account if we leave?

Yes — always. The account, its history, audiences, and data are yours from day one; we work inside it with managed access. Agency-owned accounts are a lock-in mechanism, and we consider them a red flag anywhere in this industry.

What about Performance Max campaigns?

Useful with guardrails, risky without. Automated campaign types can find efficient volume, but in healthcare they need strict creative control, brand exclusions, and conversion signals clean enough to steer them. We test them deliberately against search campaigns — never as the default.
