## Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from prospective and current partners.

How quickly will I hear back?

Most inquiries submitted through this form or sent to info@medtech4solutions.com receive a response within one business day. If your message arrives after-hours or over a weekend, expect a reply by the next business morning. For urgent laboratory or compliance matters tied to an active engagement, contact your assigned account manager directly.

Do you offer free initial consultations?

Yes. Initial discovery calls are complimentary — typically 30 to 60 minutes — and are designed to understand your goals, current state, and whether MedTech is the right fit. There is no obligation, no sales pressure, and no fee. Many of our long-term partnerships have started this way.

Can I share patient information when describing my situation?

**Please do not include protected health information (PHI) or any patient-identifying details in this form or in initial emails.** Web forms and unencrypted email are not appropriate channels for PHI. Once we begin a formal engagement, we will set up secure, HIPAA-aligned communication channels and, where applicable, execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) before any PHI is exchanged.

How does GPO membership work, and is there a fee to inquire?

MedTech's Group Purchasing Organization is free to join with no purchasing obligations or annual minimums. To explore membership, simply select “GPO Purchasing” in the form above or email gpo@medtech4solutions.com. We'll walk you through the catalog of 1,800+ active vendor contracts and the membership agreement — no commitment required to receive a full overview.

Do you serve practices outside the United States?

Yes. While our headquarters is in New York and the majority of our clients are U.S.-based, we also support international practices through laboratory design, monitoring, staffing, and practice development consulting. Contact us to discuss how our services apply to your location.

I'm a job candidate — should I use this form?

If you are an embryologist or laboratory director interested in temporary placement, please visit our [Staffing Solutions](/services/lab-solutions/staffing-solutions/) page or email staffing@medtech4solutions.com directly with your CV.

Are there services not listed that you might still help with?

Quite possibly. [Our 12 service areas](/services/) cover the most-requested needs of fertility practices, but we routinely take on adjacent projects — lab equipment validation, vendor evaluations, clinical workflow audits, second-opinion reviews, expert witness work, and more. Describe what you need in your message and we'll let you know whether it fits our scope or refer you elsewhere.
