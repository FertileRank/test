Fertility Clinic Marketing, Explained

Practical, compliance-aware guides on fertility clinic marketing — SEO, Google Ads, reputation, website conversion, and patient intake — written for the people who run US fertility clinics and donor programs.

What is fertility clinic marketing?

Fertility clinic marketing is the system of channels a clinic uses to turn patient demand into booked consultations: search visibility (SEO and local), paid acquisition (Google Ads), trust (reviews and physician-reviewed content), website conversion, and intake follow-up. Because fertility is a YMYL ("Your Money or Your Life") category, effective marketing must also satisfy stricter trust, privacy, and compliance standards than ordinary industries.

What are the six disciplines of fertility clinic marketing?

Each discipline fixes a different leak in the patient pipeline. Strong programs run them as one system, measured by consultations.
