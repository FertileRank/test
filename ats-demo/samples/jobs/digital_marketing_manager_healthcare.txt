Digital Marketing Manager — Multi-Location Healthcare Group

Digital Marketing Manager to own patient acquisition across all locations.
You will lead SEO, paid media, and conversion optimization, partner with
providers and operations, and report performance to the executive team.

- Proven management of Google Ads campaigns and paid media budgets
- Fluency with Google Analytics (GA4), Google Search Console, and dashboarding
- Content marketing strategy and execution for YMYL/healthcare topics
- Conversion rate optimization (CRO) and landing page testing experience
- Email marketing and marketing automation experience
- Reputation management / review strategy across locations
- Team leadership: ability to coach specialists and manage agencies
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or a related field

Preferred qualifications:
- Experience with HIPAA-conscious/privacy-conscious measurement
- Looker Studio dashboard development
- Meta Ads for local patient acquisition
- Structured data / schema.org implementation
- CRM experience (HubSpot or Salesforce)
- WordPress and CMS platform administration

Responsibilities:
- Own the patient-acquisition funnel from search visibility to booked appointment
- Direct website redesigns and migrations without losing organic traffic
- Build executive KPI reporting and attribution across locations
- Manage vendor and agency relationships and the paid media budget
