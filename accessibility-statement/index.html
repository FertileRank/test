Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

FertileRank wants every visitor — including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology — to be able to use this site. Our target is conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

Measures we take

Accessibility is built into how each page of this site is constructed:

  • Semantic HTML with one h1 per page and a logical heading order.
  • Full keyboard navigation for every interactive element, in a predictable order.
  • A "skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Visible focus states on links, buttons, and form fields.
  • A contrast-checked color palette: body text and orange accents used for text meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio on their backgrounds.
  • Reduced-motion support: animations respect the prefers-reduced-motion setting in your operating system or browser.
  • Visible labels on every form field, and alt text on meaningful images.

Known limitations

This site is under active development, and pages are added and revised regularly. New or recently changed pages may contain issues we have not yet caught — for example, a heading level, label, or contrast pairing that slipped through review. We test pages as they are built and re-check existing pages on a recurring schedule, but we would rather be honest that gaps can exist than claim the site is perfect.

Feedback

If any part of this site is difficult to use with assistive technology, we want to know. Email hello@fertilerank.com or call (800) 555-1234. Telling us the page, the assistive technology you were using, and what went wrong helps us reproduce and fix the issue faster. You can also reach us through the contact page.

Our remediation commitment

We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within two business days. Barriers that block core tasks — reading a page, submitting a form, reaching contact information — are treated as priority fixes and scheduled into the next site update. Lower-impact issues are logged and addressed in our regular review cycle, and we retest each fix with keyboard and screen reader before considering it resolved.
