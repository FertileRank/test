Who we are

An independent, patient-centered fertility practice serving all of Ohio

SpringCreek Fertility was founded in 2014 by Dr. Jeremy Groll with a simple commitment: reproductive medicine that is both clinically excellent and deeply human. Today, our reproductive endocrinology team cares for patients across the Dayton, Columbus, and Cincinnati metros — and from neighboring communities in Indiana and Kentucky.

A reproductive endocrinologist is a physician with advanced training in Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility (REI) — the field focused on the hormones and conditions that affect the ability to conceive. As an independent practice with our own on-site in vitro fertilization (IVF) laboratory, we keep care personal, explanations clear, and decisions centered on what is right for you.

We believe family looks different for everyone. Whether you are an individual, a couple, an LGBTQ+ family, or a single parent by choice, you will find inclusive care and compassionate support at every step.

On-site IVF laboratory

Your care stays under one roof

SpringCreek operates its own IVF laboratory, led by a doctoral-level laboratory director. Keeping embryology in-house means your care team and our scientists work side by side — coordinating egg retrievals, fertilization, embryo culture, freezing, and genetic-testing logistics with close communication at every step.

Genetic testing, including preimplantation genetic testing (PGT), is coordinated through our embryology laboratory in partnership with accredited reference labs.

Answers to common questions

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find a fertility clinic near Dayton, Columbus, or Cincinnati?

SpringCreek Fertility has three Ohio locations: Dayton (7095 Clyo Road, Centerville 45459), Columbus (6760 Avery-Muirfield Drive, Suite A, Dublin 43016), and Cincinnati (9313 Mason Montgomery Road, Mason 45040). We also welcome patients from nearby Indiana and Kentucky.

How much does IVF cost in Ohio?

Costs depend on your treatment plan and medications. We offer up-front, itemized pricing and a financial counselor who reviews your insurance benefits with you. In vitro fertilization (IVF) cycle pricing starts in a defined range, with medications billed separately. See our cost & financing options for details.

Does SpringCreek have its own IVF laboratory?

Yes. SpringCreek operates an on-site IVF laboratory led by a doctoral-level laboratory director, so egg retrieval, fertilization, embryo culture, and freezing are coordinated closely with your care team.

Is SpringCreek Fertility LGBTQ+ inclusive?

Yes. We provide inclusive care for diverse family structures, including LGBTQ+ individuals and couples and single parents by choice. Learn more on our LGBTQIA+ family building page.

What happens at my first fertility appointment?

Your first visit begins with a thorough review of your reproductive health, medical history, and goals with a board-certified reproductive endocrinologist, who then explains your options in clear, plain language.

When should I see a fertility specialist?

A common guideline is to seek an evaluation after 12 months of trying to conceive, or after 6 months if you are age 35 or older. Consider reaching out sooner if you have irregular cycles, known reproductive conditions, or questions about preserving fertility.

Do I need a referral to see a reproductive endocrinologist?

Many patients schedule directly without a referral, though some insurance plans request one. Our team can help you check your plan's requirements before your visit.

Which services does SpringCreek offer?

IVF & ICSI, IUI, egg freezing and fertility preservation, donor egg/sperm and embryo programs, gestational carrier support, PGT coordination, and LGBTQIA+ family building. Explore all treatments →
