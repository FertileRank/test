Frequently Asked Questions

What happens on the strategy call?

It's a 30-minute working session, not a demo. We review your search visibility, paid efficiency, and intake conversion, then outline what we'd fix first — in writing, either way.

Is the strategy call free?

Yes — no cost, no obligation, no hard sell. We propose a next step only when the audit shows a real fit; high-pressure sales is a failure mode FertileRank was built to avoid.

Is this the free growth audit?

It's where the audit starts. Pick a Growth priority on the form and we bring a first read on that channel to the call, then follow up in writing.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Your website URL and a rough sense of priority are enough; access to Google Business Profile, Ads, or analytics lets us go deeper. Please don't share patient or medical information.

Do you work with clinics outside the US?

Our playbooks are built around US search behavior, review platforms, and privacy obligations, so US fertility clinics are our focus. Outside the US? Send a note anyway — we'll answer quickly.
