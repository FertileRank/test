Clinical Team

The RMA of Michigan clinical team, support at every step.

Behind every physician is a coordinated clinical team. At RMA of Michigan, fertility nurses, care coordinators, medical assistants, and counselors work together to guide you through testing, treatment, and the questions in between.

In brief

Who is on the RMA of Michigan clinical team?

The clinical team includes fertility nurses, care coordinators, medical assistants, and financial counselors who work alongside the board-certified reproductive endocrinologists. Together they handle scheduling, monitoring, medication teaching, and day-to-day communication, so you always have someone to turn to during your fertility journey.

Quick answer

Your physician leads your care, but you will get to know the broader team too, the nurses and coordinators who help with monitoring, medications, and questions along the way.

Individualized care

Coordinated care, centered on you

Fertility treatment involves many moving parts, from testing and monitoring to medications and procedures. A coordinated team helps it all fit together, so you are not left to navigate the details alone.

Clear communication is a priority. The team helps you understand each step, what to expect next, and who to contact with questions between visits.

This page is informational. For specific clinical questions, your physician and nursing team are the best resource.

Frequently asked

Common questions

Who will I work with during treatment?

Alongside your physician, you will work with fertility nurses, care coordinators, and medical assistants, plus financial counselors and patient-services staff.

Who do I call with questions between visits?

Your nursing team and care coordinators are your main contacts for clinical and scheduling questions between visits.

Who handles my medications and monitoring?

Fertility nurses guide medication teaching and coordinate your monitoring visits with the physician.

Who helps with costs?

Financial counselors help with estimates, verifying benefits, and planning for costs.

Is care really coordinated?

Yes. The team works together so testing, monitoring, medications, and procedures fit together smoothly.

How do I get started?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment to begin.
