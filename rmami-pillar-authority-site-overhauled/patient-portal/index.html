Patient portal access for fertility care coordination without extra friction

Resource pages should help patients complete the next administrative or care step with less back-and-forth.

The refreshed structure groups patient portal, forms, monitoring, insurance, financing, bill pay, and scheduling pathways in a consistent format.

Privacy-sensitive items are framed carefully so patients know when to use secure channels instead of sending medical or account details through general contact paths.

Fastest routeUse the linked resource if the question is about forms, portal access, monitoring, billing, or financing.
Secure routeUse protected channels for records, clinical details, or account-specific questions.
Human routeCall (248) 619-3100 when timing, symptoms, procedures, or instructions are unclear.

What to have ready

  • Patient name, date of birth, phone number, and the specific question you need answered.
  • Insurance card, prior records, medication list, referral information, or account details when relevant.
  • Secure portal access for medical records or protected health information.
