About the Practice

About RMA of Michigan, fertility care built on trust since 2006.

Reproductive Medicine Associates of Michigan (RMA of Michigan) has helped individuals, couples, and families grow since 2006. From our Troy clinic, our board-certified reproductive endocrinologists pair advanced reproductive technology with compassionate, individualized care for patients across Michigan and Ohio.

In brief

Who is RMA of Michigan?

RMA of Michigan is a fertility clinic in Troy, Michigan, founded in 2006 by Dr. Brad T. Miller and Dr. Lynda J. Wolf. The practice now includes five board-certified reproductive endocrinologists and offers IVF, IUI, egg freezing, donor programs, genetic testing, and inclusive family-building care. RMA of Michigan is a member of the Society for Assisted Reproductive Technology (SART) and the American Society for Reproductive Medicine (ASRM).

Quick answer

Our mission is simple: combine clinical expertise with genuine compassion to support every patient's path to parenthood. We have helped welcome thousands of babies, and we treat each journey as unique.

Individualized care

Expertise and empathy, together

Fertility care is personal, and it deserves both technical excellence and genuine warmth. RMA of Michigan blends advanced reproductive technology with a deeply human approach, taking time to explain options and answer questions in plain language.

Our physicians are board-certified reproductive endocrinologists with deep experience in IVF, fertility preservation, recurrent pregnancy loss, donor programs, and inclusive family building. Behind them stands a coordinated team of nurses, embryologists, financial counselors, and patient-services staff.

We are proud to have supported thousands of families since 2006. Every patient's story is different, and we are honored to be part of it.

Frequently asked

Common questions

When was RMA of Michigan founded?

RMA of Michigan was founded in 2006 by Dr. Brad T. Miller and Dr. Lynda J. Wolf, and has grown to a team of five board-certified reproductive endocrinologists.

Where is RMA of Michigan located?

The main clinic is at 130 Town Center Drive, Suite 106, Troy, MI 48084, convenient to Oakland County and metro Detroit. A Livonia location is planned.

Is the practice board-certified and accredited?

The physicians are board-certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology, and the practice is a member of SART and ASRM.

Does RMA of Michigan treat all families?

Yes. Care is inclusive of LGBTQ+ patients, single parents by choice, and individuals and couples building their families.

How many families has RMA of Michigan helped?

The practice has supported thousands of families since 2006. Every journey is unique, and outcomes vary by individual.

How do I become a patient?
