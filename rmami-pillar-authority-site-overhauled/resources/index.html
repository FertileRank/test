In brief

What patient resources does RMA of Michigan offer?

RMA of Michigan offers practical resources to support your journey: patient forms, the secure patient portal, educational guides and videos, financial planning tools (insurance, financing, grants, and medication savings), and a clinic FAQ. These resources help you prepare for visits, understand your options, and stay organized.

Quick answer

Use the links below to find forms, log in to the portal, learn about treatments, or plan for costs. If you are not sure where to start, the fertility guide is a great first stop.

Frequently asked

Common questions

Where should I start if I am a new patient?

The fertility guide is a great first stop, and the clinic FAQ answers common questions. Patient services can help you take the next step.

How do I complete patient forms?

You can complete forms ahead of your visit, including through the secure patient portal. Patient services can help if you have trouble.

What is the patient portal?

The portal offers secure online access to forms, messages, and parts of your care. It helps you stay connected with the team.

Where can I learn about treatments?

The treatments pages and patient education videos explain options such as IVF, IUI, and egg freezing in plain language.

How do I plan for costs?

The insurance, financing, grants, and medication savings pages help you plan, and financial counselors can assist.

How do I contact the clinic?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment online.
