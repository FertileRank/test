Troy Fertility Clinic

Our fertility clinic in Troy, MI, care close to home.

RMA of Michigan's main clinic is in Troy, at 130 Town Center Drive, Suite 106, convenient to Oakland County and metro Detroit. Here you will find consultations, testing, monitoring, procedures, and a coordinated care team, all in one welcoming location.

In brief

Where is RMA of Michigan in Troy?

RMA of Michigan is located at 130 Town Center Drive, Suite 106, Troy, MI 48084, in Oakland County. The clinic serves patients across metro Detroit and nearby communities including Birmingham, Royal Oak, Bloomfield Hills, Rochester Hills, and Novi. You can reach the office at (248) 619-3100.

Quick answer

The Troy clinic offers consultations, fertility testing, morning monitoring, egg retrievals, and embryo transfers. Call (248) 619-3100 to schedule, and parking is available on site.

Individualized care

A welcoming home for your fertility care

Having testing, monitoring, and procedures in one coordinated location makes the fertility journey smoother. The Troy clinic is designed to be efficient and supportive, with early-morning monitoring hours that fit busy schedules.

Many patients travel from across Oakland County and metro Detroit, and the team also supports out-of-town and Ohio patients. A Livonia location is planned to expand access on the west side.
