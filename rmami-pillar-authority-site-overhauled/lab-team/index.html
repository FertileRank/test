Laboratory Team

The RMA of Michigan laboratory team, science behind your care.

The fertility laboratory is where much of IVF happens. At RMA of Michigan, experienced embryologists and andrology staff care for eggs, sperm, and embryos with precision, working closely with your physician throughout your cycle.

In brief

What does the fertility laboratory team do?

The laboratory team includes embryologists and andrology staff who handle the delicate scientific steps of IVF: preparing sperm, fertilizing eggs (including ICSI, intracytoplasmic sperm injection, when appropriate), culturing embryos, and freezing eggs or embryos for later use. They follow careful quality and safety practices and coordinate closely with your physician.

Quick answer

While you may not meet the laboratory team in person, their work is central to IVF, fertilization, embryo development, and cryopreservation, all handled with care and rigorous standards.

Inside the lab

What the laboratory team handles

Precise, careful work supports every IVF cycle.

Embryology

Fertilizing eggs and culturing embryos through the stages of development.

Andrology

Preparing and evaluating sperm samples for IUI and IVF.

ICSI

Placing a single sperm into an egg when sperm factors make it appropriate.

Cryopreservation

Freezing eggs and embryos by vitrification and managing secure storage.

Genetic testing coordination

Biopsying embryos and coordinating samples for genetics laboratories.

Quality and safety

Following careful protocols and identity checks at every step.

Individualized care

Precision that supports your plan

IVF depends on skilled laboratory work. The embryology team cares for eggs and embryos through fertilization and development, and the andrology team prepares and evaluates sperm. Each step follows established protocols.

The laboratory works hand in hand with your physician. Decisions about fertilization methods, embryo culture, freezing, and genetic testing are coordinated so your plan stays consistent.

This page is informational. Your physician can explain how the laboratory steps apply to your specific treatment.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What does an embryologist do?

Embryologists fertilize eggs, culture embryos, and manage freezing and storage during IVF, following careful protocols and identity checks.

What is the andrology team?

The andrology team prepares and evaluates sperm samples for IUI and IVF, including for use with ICSI when appropriate.

How are embryos frozen?

Embryos and eggs are frozen by vitrification, a fast-freezing method, and stored securely until they are needed.

Is genetic testing done in the lab?

The laboratory biopsies embryos when PGT is planned and coordinates the samples with a genetics laboratory for analysis.

How does the lab keep samples safe?

The team follows established quality, safety, and identity-verification practices at each step.

Will I meet the laboratory team?

You may not meet them in person, but their work is central to your care and coordinated with your physician.
