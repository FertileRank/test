Grants

Fertility grants in Michigan, help offsetting the cost of care.

Grants can help offset the cost of fertility treatment. RMA of Michigan shares grant opportunities, including the Christen Goff IVF Grant for Michigan and Ohio residents, along with national resources, so you can explore every avenue of support.

In brief

What fertility grants are available?

Fertility grants are offered by nonprofit organizations and programs to help patients with the cost of treatment, often through an application process. RMA of Michigan highlights the Christen Goff IVF Grant, available to Michigan and Ohio residents, and can point you toward national grant resources. Eligibility, timing, and award amounts vary by program.

Quick answer

Grants typically require an application and have eligibility criteria. The Christen Goff IVF Grant is currently open to Michigan and Ohio residents; ask our team how to learn more and apply.

Individualized care

Making the most of grant opportunities

Grants are competitive and have specific eligibility rules, so it helps to research carefully and apply early. Our team can point you toward current opportunities, including the Christen Goff IVF Grant, and explain how grants fit alongside other financial options.

Award decisions, amounts, and timelines are set by each grant program, not by the clinic. We cannot promise an award, but we can help you find and pursue opportunities.

This page is educational. Always review each program's official eligibility criteria and deadlines before applying.

Planning for cost and coverage

Grants are one part of a financial plan that may also include insurance, financing, and medication savings.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What is the Christen Goff IVF Grant?

It is an IVF grant currently open to Michigan and Ohio residents. Ask our team for current details, eligibility, and how to apply.

Who offers fertility grants?

Grants come from nonprofit organizations and programs, some national and some focused on specific situations, such as fertility preservation before cancer treatment.

How do I apply for a grant?

Most grants have an application and eligibility criteria. Review each program's official requirements and deadlines, and our team can help point you to resources.

Can grants guarantee I get treatment funded?

No. Award decisions and amounts are made by each program, and grants are competitive. They are one option among several to explore.

Can I combine a grant with other options?

Yes. Grants can be paired with insurance, financing, and medication savings as part of an overall plan.

Where do I start?

Call (248) 619-3100 or ask at your consultation about current grant opportunities, including the Christen Goff IVF Grant.
