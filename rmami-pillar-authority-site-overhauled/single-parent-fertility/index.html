Single Parent Fertility

Single parent fertility in Michigan, building your family on your terms.

Choosing to become a parent on your own is a meaningful path, and RMA of Michigan is here to support it. Depending on your goals, care may include donor sperm IUI, IVF, donor eggs, or fertility preservation, with clear guidance at every step.

In brief

How can single people build a family with fertility care?

Single parents by choice have several options. Many begin with donor sperm IUI (intrauterine insemination) or IVF (in vitro fertilization) using donor sperm, donor eggs, or both, depending on their needs. Fertility preservation, such as egg freezing, is also an option for planning ahead. Care is individualized, and the team helps you understand testing, donor choices, and timelines.

Quick answer

Your first step is a consultation to discuss your goals, any testing, and donor needs. The team coordinates the medical care and helps connect you with recommended donor and legal resources.

Your options

Paths for single parents by choice

There is no single right path. Your physician helps you choose based on your goals and history.

Donor sperm IUI

Prepared donor sperm is placed into the uterus around ovulation, often a first-step option.

IVF

Eggs are retrieved, fertilized with donor sperm in the laboratory, and an embryo is transferred or frozen.

Donor eggs

Screened donor eggs can support family building when needed.

Fertility preservation

Egg freezing can help keep options open while you plan.

Donor selection support

Guidance through choosing screened donor sperm or eggs.

Whole-journey support

Coordination of testing, treatment, and recommended legal steps.

Individualized care

Supportive, individualized care for solo parents

Becoming a single parent by choice is a thoughtful decision, and your plan should reflect your goals and history. The team explains options, donor considerations, testing, and timelines in clear, respectful language.

Using donor sperm or eggs involves screening and recommended legal steps. The team can help connect you with trusted donor resources and reproductive attorneys.

This page is educational and does not replace individualized medical or legal advice. A consultation is the best place to map out your path.

Frequently asked

Common questions

Can I have a baby on my own with fertility care?

Yes. Many single parents by choice build families using donor sperm IUI or IVF with donor sperm, donor eggs, or both. Care is individualized to your goals.

Is donor sperm IUI a good first step?

For many single parents, donor sperm IUI is a reasonable first option. Your physician will help determine whether IUI or IVF fits your situation.

How do I choose a sperm or egg donor?

Donors are screened, and the team helps you understand options and the recommended legal steps as you choose.

Should I consider egg freezing?

If you are planning for the future, egg freezing can help keep options open. Ovarian reserve testing helps with timing.

What legal steps are recommended?

Using donor gametes typically involves legal guidance. The team can connect you with experienced reproductive attorneys.

How do I get started?

Begin with a consultation. The team will discuss your goals, any testing, and a plan that fits you.
