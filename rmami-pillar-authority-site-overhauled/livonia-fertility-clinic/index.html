Livonia Location (Opening Soon)

A new RMA of Michigan fertility clinic, coming to Livonia.

RMA of Michigan is expanding to Livonia at the Corewell Health Care Center, bringing fertility care closer to patients on the west side of metro Detroit. In the meantime, our Troy clinic welcomes patients from Livonia and surrounding communities.

In brief

Is RMA of Michigan opening a Livonia clinic?

Yes. RMA of Michigan is planning a Livonia location at the Corewell Health Care Center to expand access on the west side of metro Detroit. Until it opens, patients from Livonia, Plymouth, Canton, Northville, and Westland are warmly cared for at the Troy clinic. Call (248) 619-3100 to learn more or to schedule.

Quick answer

The Livonia location is planned; details and the opening date will be confirmed. For now, the Troy clinic at 130 Town Center Drive serves west-side patients, and the team can help you get started.

Bringing fertility care closer to home

Expanding to Livonia is part of RMA of Michigan's commitment to making fertility care more accessible across the region. The planned Corewell Health Care Center location will serve communities on the west side of metro Detroit.

While the Livonia clinic is being prepared, the Troy clinic provides the full range of services, and the team is happy to help west-side patients begin their journey now.

This page is informational. The location address and opening date will be confirmed; please contact the team for the latest details.

Frequently asked

Common questions

Where will the Livonia clinic be located?

It is planned at the Corewell Health Care Center in Livonia. The exact suite and opening date will be confirmed.

When does the Livonia clinic open?

An opening date will be announced. In the meantime, the Troy clinic serves west-side patients.

Which areas will the Livonia clinic serve?

It is intended to serve Livonia, Plymouth, Canton, Northville, Westland, and western Wayne County.

Can I be seen before Livonia opens?

Yes. The Troy clinic at 130 Town Center Drive welcomes patients now, including those from the west side.

Will the same doctors practice in Livonia?

Yes. Patients will see the same board-certified reproductive endocrinologists and experience the same care standards.

How do I get more information?

Call (248) 619-3100 or ask the team for the latest on the Livonia location.
