Patient Services

Patient services, help that keeps your journey on track.

From your first call to ongoing visits, the RMA of Michigan patient services team helps with scheduling, records, billing questions, and the practical details, so you can focus on your care.

Patient services MichiganTroy, Michigan
Schedule Your Consultation Call (248) 619-3100
In brief

How does patient services help?

The patient services team is your point of contact for scheduling appointments, managing records and forms, answering billing questions, and helping you get started. They work behind the scenes to keep communication clear and your visits coordinated, so the logistics of fertility care are easier to manage.

Quick answer

Need to schedule, update records, or ask a billing question? Patient services can help. Call (248) 619-3100 or use the patient portal to stay connected.

The practical support behind good care

Fertility care involves a lot of coordination. The patient services team helps manage scheduling, records, and communication so the details do not add to your stress.

Whether you are a new patient figuring out where to start or an established patient juggling monitoring visits, patient services is there to help things run smoothly.

This page is informational. For clinical questions, your nursing team and physician are the best resource.

Frequently asked

Common questions

How do I schedule or change an appointment?

Call (248) 619-3100 or use the patient portal. Patient services will help you book or reschedule.

How do I complete forms or request records?

Patient services can guide you through forms and records requests, including via the patient portal.

Who do I ask about billing?

Patient services can answer general questions and connect you with financial counselors for benefits and estimates.

How do I get started as a new patient?

Patient services helps new patients with first steps, scheduling, and what to bring to the first visit.

What is the patient portal?

The patient portal offers secure online access to forms, messages, and parts of your care. Patient services can help you get set up.

How do I reach patient services?

Call (248) 619-3100 during business hours or message through the patient portal.
