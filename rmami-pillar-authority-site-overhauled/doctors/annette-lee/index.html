About Dr. Annette Lee

Background

More than two decades in reproductive medicine

With over 27 years of experience, Dr. Lee has cared for patients through a wide range of fertility situations and reproductive endocrine conditions.

Her recognition as a Castle Connolly Top Doctor reflects peer review within the field; in the exam room, that experience shows up as clear explanations and realistic expectation-setting.

A visit with Dr. Lee starts with your history and goals, followed by a discussion of which testing or treatment options fit your situation and timeline.

What to bring to a first visit

  • Prior fertility records, labs, and imaging
  • A list of current medications and supplements
  • Questions about IVF or reproductive endocrine concerns
  • Insurance information for benefit review
