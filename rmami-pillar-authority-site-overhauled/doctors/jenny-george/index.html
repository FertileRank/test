About Dr. Jenny S. George

Dr. Jenny S. George is a reproductive endocrinologist at RMA of Michigan who joined the Troy practice in 2023. She completed reproductive endocrinology and infertility fellowship training in the Harvard system and cares for patients across the full range of fertility evaluation, IVF, and egg freezing.

Background

Fellowship-trained reproductive endocrinology

Dr. George completed subspecialty fellowship training in reproductive endocrinology and infertility within the Harvard system before joining RMA of Michigan in 2023.

Her practice spans fertility testing, IVF, and fertility preservation such as egg freezing, with an emphasis on helping patients understand what each result does and does not tell them.

A first visit focuses on your history, any prior testing, and your goals, followed by a discussion of evaluation or treatment options suited to your timeline.

What to bring to a first visit

  • Prior fertility records, labs, and imaging
  • A list of current medications and supplements
  • Questions about IVF, egg freezing, or testing
  • Insurance information for benefit review
Next steps

A clear path to a first appointment

  1. Request a consultation by phone or online.
  2. Gather prior records, labs, and imaging to share.
  3. Meet to review your history, testing, and options.

Frequently asked

Questions patients often ask

When did Dr. George join RMA of Michigan?

Dr. George joined the RMA of Michigan physician team in 2023. She completed reproductive endocrinology and infertility fellowship training within the Harvard system.

Does Dr. George help with egg freezing?

Yes. Dr. George cares for patients interested in fertility preservation, including egg freezing, alongside fertility evaluation and IVF.

How do I schedule an appointment at RMA of Michigan?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment online. The scheduling team will help confirm the visit type, location, and next steps.

Where is RMA of Michigan located?

The main clinic is at 130 Town Center Drive, Suite 106, Troy, MI 48084. A Livonia location is also available at the Corewell Health Care Center.
