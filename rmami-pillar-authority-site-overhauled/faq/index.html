In brief

How does care at RMA of Michigan work?

Care at RMA of Michigan begins with a consultation and fertility testing to understand your situation. From there, your physician recommends a personalized plan, which may include monitoring, ovulation induction, IUI, IVF, donor options, or fertility preservation. The team supports you through scheduling, financial planning, and each step of treatment.

Quick answer

To get started, call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment online. Bring prior records, recent labs, any semen analysis, a medication list, and your insurance card to your first visit.

Individualized care

Getting the most from your first visit

A productive first visit starts with preparation. Bring prior fertility records, recent labs and imaging, any semen analysis results, a list of medications, and your insurance card, along with your questions.

Your physician will review your history and goals, discuss testing, and outline a starting plan. You will leave knowing who to contact and what to schedule next.

This page is educational and does not replace individualized medical advice. A consultation is the best place to discuss your specific situation.

Frequently asked

Common questions

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment online. The scheduling team will confirm the visit type, location, and next steps.

Where is RMA of Michigan located?

The main clinic is at 130 Town Center Drive, Suite 106, Troy, MI 48084. A Livonia location is planned at the Corewell Health Care Center.

What are your hours?

The clinic is generally open Monday through Thursday 6:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Friday 6:00 AM to 1:00 PM, and weekends 6:00 AM to 9:30 AM for monitoring. Confirm current hours when you schedule.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring prior fertility records, recent labs and imaging, any semen analysis, a medication list, your insurance card, and your questions.

Do I need a referral?

Some insurance plans require a referral and some do not. Check with your plan, and the team can help confirm what is needed.

Does RMA of Michigan accept insurance?

The practice works with many plans, and coverage varies. The financial team helps verify your benefits before treatment begins.

Do you care for LGBTQ+ patients and single parents?

Yes. Care is inclusive of LGBTQ+ patients, single parents by choice, and individuals and couples building their families.

How soon can I be seen?

Availability varies. Call (248) 619-3100 to ask about the next available consultation.
