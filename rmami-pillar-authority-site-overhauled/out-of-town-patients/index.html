Out-of-Town Patients

Out-of-town fertility care, coordinated to fit your travel.

Choosing the right fertility team can be worth a trip. RMA of Michigan helps out-of-town and out-of-state patients plan efficient visits, coordinate local monitoring where possible, and stay supported throughout treatment.

In brief

How does RMA of Michigan support out-of-town patients?

RMA of Michigan helps patients who travel for care by coordinating scheduling to reduce trips, arranging some monitoring closer to home when possible, and planning key steps such as egg retrieval and embryo transfer efficiently. The team also helps with practical questions about timing and logistics, so distance is less of a barrier.

Quick answer

Many steps can be planned around your travel. Some early monitoring may be done locally, with key procedures scheduled at the Troy clinic. The team helps map out a realistic plan.

How it works

How travel-friendly care works

A clear plan keeps trips to a minimum and reduces stress.

  1. Virtual or initial consultation

    Begin with a consultation to review your history and outline a plan.

  2. Local testing and records

    Where possible, complete some testing locally and share records with the team.

  3. Coordinated monitoring

    Some monitoring may be arranged closer to home during stimulation.

  4. Key visits in Troy

    Procedures such as egg retrieval and embryo transfer are scheduled at the clinic.

  5. Travel planning

    The team helps you time trips and plan logistics.

  6. Ongoing support

    Stay connected with the care team between visits.

Individualized care

Distance does not have to be a barrier

Patients travel for many reasons, from seeking a particular team to living in a community without nearby fertility care. RMA of Michigan plans visits thoughtfully so travel is manageable and time is used well.

Coordination is key. The team works with you, and sometimes with a local provider, to handle monitoring efficiently and schedule the steps that must happen in Troy.

This page is informational. Talk with the team about what can be coordinated locally for your specific plan.

Questions to bring

Cost & coverage

Planning for cost and coverage

Costs depend on your treatment and insurance, and the team can review benefits regardless of where you live.

The RMA of Michigan financial team helps verify your benefits, provide a written estimate, and review financing before treatment begins, so you can plan with fewer surprises. Patients can also explore the Christen Goff IVF Grant and ask about IVF medication savings.

Financial next steps

  • Verify your insurance benefits
  • Compare financing options
  • Explore grants such as the Christen Goff IVF Grant
  • Ask about medication savings

Frequently asked

Common questions

Can I do fertility treatment if I live far away?

Yes. Many out-of-town patients receive care with coordinated scheduling, some local monitoring, and key visits planned at the Troy clinic.

Which steps must happen in Troy?

Procedures such as egg retrieval and embryo transfer take place at the clinic. Some monitoring and testing may be done closer to home.

Can monitoring be done near where I live?

In many cases, yes. The team can coordinate with a local provider for some monitoring during your cycle.

How do I begin if I am out of state?

Start with a consultation. The team will outline a plan and discuss what can be coordinated locally.

Will the team help with travel timing?

Yes. The team helps you plan trips around the steps that require a visit, to limit travel.

How do I get started?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment to plan travel-friendly care.
