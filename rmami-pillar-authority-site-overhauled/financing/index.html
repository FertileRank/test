Financing Options

Fertility financing in Michigan, options to make care manageable.

When insurance does not cover everything, financing can help spread the cost of fertility care over time. RMA of Michigan reviews financing options, payment planning, and written estimates so you can move forward with a clear picture.

In brief

How can I finance fertility treatment?

Several options can help make fertility treatment more manageable, including fertility-specific loans, healthcare credit programs, payment planning, and grants. The right approach depends on your situation. The RMA of Michigan financial team provides a written estimate and walks you through the options so you can plan with confidence.

Quick answer

Start with a written estimate and a benefits check. From there, the financial team helps you compare financing, payment planning, grants, and medication savings.

Individualized care

Planning that fits your situation

Cost should not be the first thing that stops you from getting information. The financial team helps you understand the full picture, from insurance and estimates to financing, grants, and savings, before you commit to treatment.

Because every situation is different, we walk through options rather than apply a single formula. You decide what fits your budget and goals, with clear numbers in hand.

This page is educational and does not constitute financial or lending advice. Terms for any financing program are set by the provider of that program.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What financing options are available?

Options can include fertility-specific loans, healthcare credit programs, and payment planning. The financial team helps you compare them.

Can I get a cost estimate before deciding?

Yes. After verifying benefits, the team provides a written estimate so you can plan before committing.

Do you offer payment plans?

Structured payment options may be available depending on your situation. Ask the financial team for details.

Are there grants that can help?

Grant programs, including the Christen Goff IVF Grant for Michigan and Ohio residents, may help. Eligibility and availability vary.

How can I lower medication costs?

Specialty pharmacy and savings programs can help. Ask about medication savings before stimulation medications are ordered.

How do I get started?

Call (248) 619-3100 or request an appointment to discuss estimates and options.
